The Night Shift Turnover Crisis

passage_id: passage-c009

The digital dashboard in Plant Four flashes red: three packaging operators on line two walked out before midnight, marking the fifth departure this week. Marco, the plant operations manager, stares at the empty stations while dough mixers run at maximum velocity. The shift supervisor, Elena, approaches with her clipboard. She explains that the new hires cannot handle the relentless pace and the repetitive strain of lifting heavy trays during the first grueling weeks. Marco knows that hiring replacements takes two weeks, yet the factory cannot afford to slow down production to train people properly. He needs to decide whether to temporarily reduce line speed to protect new operators or maintain output and risk losing the entire shift to exhaustion. The clock is ticking toward the morning delivery deadline, and the next batch of dough is already in the hopper.
