The Midnight Shortage

passage_id: art-01

At 11:00 PM, the head of procurement at Metropolis Foods received an urgent alert: the primary supplier for organic vanilla extract had suffered a catastrophic warehouse fire, halting all shipments for the next month. The flagship dessert line, representing forty percent of quarterly revenue, was scheduled to start production on Monday morning.

Two options lay on the table. The first was to source an unvetted distributor three cities away who promised immediate delivery but lacked standard compliance documentation. The second was to halt production, notify retail partners of a massive delay, and protect the brand's strict quality reputation at a severe financial cost. The plant director stared at the blinking terminal, knowing that every hour of indecision would compound the loss.
