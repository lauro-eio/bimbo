The Overlap Crisis at Apex Consumer Goods

passage_id: p01

The conference room on the tenth floor was silent except for the hum of the projector. Elena, the VP of Marketing, stared at the latest quarterly figures. Her flagship skincare brand, Lumina, was seeing flat growth, while their newly acquired mid-tier brand, GlowAll, was surging past targets. The problem was glaringly obvious. GlowAll had quietly lowered its retail price and introduced a vitamin serum that looked, felt, and performed identically to Lumina's hero product, but at half the cost.

Sales data confirmed the worst fear: loyal Lumina customers were trading down to GlowAll inside the same retail stores. Elena's two brand managers sat across the table from each other, both defending their turf. The GlowAll director argued that capturing budget-conscious shoppers was a win for total corporate revenue. Meanwhile, the Lumina lead insisted that the brand equity built over a decade was eroding fast. Elena had twenty-four hours to present a brand differentiation strategy to the executive committee before the annual retailer summit.
