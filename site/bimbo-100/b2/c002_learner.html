The Boardroom Pitch

passage_id: art_001

The projector hummed quietly in the quiet meeting room as Marcus pointed to the final slide. On the screen, a clean upward curve showed projected output surging past historical baselines once the new high-speed packaging machinery went live. The production floor was currently choked by bottlenecks, and error rates on the legacy belts were climbing every quarter.

Finance Director Elena crossed her arms, tapping a pen against her notepad. She did not look at the chart. Instead, her eyes were fixed on the initial capital outlay column. The equipment price tag was substantial, requiring a multi-million-dollar commitment that would absorb half the regional contingency fund for the fiscal year.

Marcus needed to defend the numbers, but he knew quoting manufacturer brochures would not convince her. He had to prove that the payback period was bulletproof before the board session tomorrow morning.
