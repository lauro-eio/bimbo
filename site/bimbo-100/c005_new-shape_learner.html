The Morning Rush at Sector Four

passage_id: dsd_vignette_01

At 6:15 AM sharp, the loading bay at District Store 402 is completely gridlocked with heavy traffic. Three regional delivery vans sit entirely idle because the primary receiving dock is packed with yesterday's unsorted inventory boxes. Mateo, the senior regional supply chain coordinator, watches the anxious store manager pace near the main entrance doors. The fresh bakery and dairy items are scheduled to unload in exactly ten minutes, but the dock team is severely understaffed and visibly overwhelmed by the backlog. Mateo's corporate phone suddenly buzzes with an automated priority alert from the central warehouse reporting yet another delayed truck stuck on the busy ring road. Instead of waiting for the afternoon audit to assign blame, Mateo steps firmly onto the concrete platform, grabs a clipboard from the counter, and pulls the shift supervisor aside to aggressively renegotiate the unloading sequence on the spot.
