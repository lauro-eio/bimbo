The Packaging Line Dilemma

passage_id: p1

The plant manager stood in front of the board with a blank slide. On his desk sat a massive proposal for new high-speed packaging machinery. The current line jammed twice a day, costing hours of lost output. Yet, the finance director crossed her arms, waiting for proof. The price tag was high, and the board wanted a clear return on investment before signing off. The manager had to convince them that spending big money now would save the factory from falling behind its competitors by next year.
