The Morning Shift Crash

passage_id: p_c003_art

Plant Manager Clara stared at her screen. The legacy inventory software froze right when the morning shift needed flour orders confirmed. Mateo from IT walked in with bad news: the old server could not connect to the new SAP software. Downstairs, the mixers stopped because workers had no digital recipes. Clara had to choose immediately. She could pause the entire bakery line to fix the system now, or she could use paper notes and risk sending wrong product counts to the central office.
