The Morning Truck

passage_id: passage_dsd_01

At 5:00 AM, the delivery truck arrives outside Store 402. The driver has twenty minutes to unload fresh bread and fruit before the morning customers walk through the doors. Inside, the store manager looks at her clipboard. Half the crates are missing, and the driver is holding a wrong invoice. The manager has to decide: accept the partial delivery and risk empty shelves by noon, or send the truck away and wait twenty-four hours for the next run. A queue of delivery vans is already forming behind the first truck, and the store staff is waiting.

Q3

Role-play Task

Host notes

Assign roles: Student A is the Store Manager, Student B is the Delivery Driver. Set a 5-minute timer to resolve the inventory discrepancy and agree on a delivery schedule.

First three

Role-play the situation between the Store Manager (Student A) and the Delivery Driver (Student B). Situation: Half the morning bread delivery is missing and the invoice is incorrect. Outcome: Agree on whether to accept a partial drop-off or reschedule.
