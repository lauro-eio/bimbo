The Silent Conveyor

passage_id: passage_01

The main conveyor belt stopped at 3:00 AM, right in the middle of a heavy night shift. The shift supervisor, Mateo, stared at the silent motor while four technicians scrambled to find spare parts. It took six hours to fix the belt, costing the plant thousands of dollars in lost products. Mateo wiped his forehead and looked at the old analog gauges on the wall. They showed normal numbers right up until the moment of failure. He muttered to his plant manager, "We need to spot early signs before things break, not wait for a complete blackout." The manager crossed his arms and sighed. "Sensors cost money, Mateo, and our budget is tight this quarter."
